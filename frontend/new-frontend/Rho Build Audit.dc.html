What's working

The bus keeps the four stages genuinely decoupled — poller, graph, scorer, and broadcaster only ever exchange the models in models.py, which has held as a stable contract across both commits. The checked-in sandbox fixtures let the graph and scorer build and test with no network and no API key, and the inject route feeds the identical pipeline the poller does, so what the demo shows is what the system does.

Four things are weak, in priority order

Most severe first.

1

The demo cannot survive rehearsal.

Re-injecting the same transaction three times scored alert → warn → normal. Three compounding causes: the vendor stops being new, its own amounts pollute its baseline, and the population mean drifts. There is no reset short of restarting uvicorn.

2

The graph does no work.

Only one feature derives from networkx and nothing reads it. Everything scored is a group-by over three dictionaries — delete the graph object and the scores don't change.

3

Over half the feed is yellow.

38 of 72 rows are warns; first contact with a vendor alone clears the threshold. The median transaction scores 0.523 against a 0.50 line.

4

Untested code sits in the critical path.

468 lines with zero test coverage, and it's exactly the code touching the outside world.

Poller, graph, cursor, and scorer are well covered — the gap is everything network- and HTTP-facing, which is also where a venue failure would hit hardest.

Correction to earlier guidance

Meridian Travel Inc. was wrongly proposed as the impersonation target. Its three transactions are all inbound wires, so Meridian pays you; impersonating a customer to extract a payment makes no sense. The right target is Crescent Property Group, paid by check twice for rent at $3,391 — an impostor invoicing a known recurring payee by wire with new bank details is the real fraud pattern.

The scenario-idempotency plan was also wrong. You can't subtract a sample from a Welford accumulator or un-pollute account/population stats, so each scenario does a full fixture reset before rerunning, making repeated clicks identical by construction rather than by arithmetic.
